Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The advent calendars at your-site.example are run by YOUR BUSINESS NAME, YOUR BUSINESS ADDRESS. This page explains what happens to your information when you open them. The short version: you don't make an account, your email is only collected if you sign up for reminders, and we don't sell anything about you.

What we collect

Opening doors needs nothing from you. No account, no name and no email.

Your email, only if you ask for reminders. The sign-up box on each calendar collects your email address, which reminders you picked, whether you also want the newsletter, and your time zone (so the morning email arrives in your morning). We send one email to confirm first, and nothing else goes out until you tap its button. We use your email only to send the reminders you picked. Every reminder has a link to stop them.

The newsletter box. If you check it and confirm, your email is added to the newsletter, which is sent through YOUR NEWSLETTER PROVIDER. From then on its privacy policy applies to the newsletter, and you can unsubscribe from any newsletter email.

How long we keep it. Advent reminders end after December 24. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after about a week, and every reminder sign-up is deleted by February 1 of the next year.

Your stamp card stays in your browser. When you open a door, the calendar saves which doors you've opened in your browser's local storage, under a key named like advent:my-calendar:2026. It's a list of door numbers and nothing else. It never leaves your device, we can't see it, and clearing your browser data erases it.

Server logs. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives the standard details any website gets when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. We use them only to keep the site running and safe from abuse, and we don't use them to identify you.

Cookies

The calendars set no cookies.

Other companies the pages talk to

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its database (Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1). The database holds the calendar doors and, if you sign up, your reminder sign-up.
  • Resend (delete this line if you don't send email) sends the reminder emails, only if you sign up for them. It receives your email address and the email we send you.
  • YOUR NEWSLETTER PROVIDER sends the newsletter, only if you check the newsletter box and confirm. (Delete this line if you don't set NEWSLETTER_NAME.)
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits so we can see how many people open the calendars. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify you or follow you to other sites. (Delete this line if you don't turn on Web Analytics for your site.)
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (delete this line if you don't turn it on) checks that a real person is using the reminder sign-up box, so bots can't fill it. It runs only when you start typing your email there, and Cloudflare uses it only for that check.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts. Your browser asks Google for them, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • Google Calendar, only if you tap the Google Calendar reminder button. That opens Google's own site, and Google's privacy policy applies there.

Some doors link to other websites. Once you leave this site, that site's privacy policy applies.

Links in the doors

Some notes under a door point to our own products, and those are labeled "My product." Door links to our own sites carry no label. Some links are affiliate links, and those are clearly labeled. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Doors paid for by a brand are marked as ads at the top of the door.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. The advent calendars don't use advertising or tracking tools that follow you across other websites, so they work the same whether or not your browser sends that signal. We don't let any third party collect information about your activity here to track you across other sites over time.

Children

The advent calendars aren't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

If you signed up for reminders, you can stop them with the link in any reminder email, or email us to see, correct or delete your sign-up. If you didn't sign up, we hold no personal data about you. Your stamp card lives in your own browser, and you can delete it any time by clearing this site's data. If you have a question about your privacy, email you@example.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top.
